Which suburbs are closest to the edge?
Policy finding: At current prices, 42 SA2s are already in High or Critical tier (relative ranking). Christie Downs, Jamestown, Kadina and others cross the absolute 4% hardship threshold at just +4.7% (the FY2025-26 increase). Coastal retirement towns — Victor Harbor, Goolwa, Moonta — are already Critical by relative vulnerability ranking (burden ratios 2.32–2.34%) and reach the absolute 4% hardship threshold between +5% and +15%. Fixed superannuation incomes mean they cannot absorb any price rise.
